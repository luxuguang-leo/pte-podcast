# PTE 备考指北 — EP01
# 城市 vs 乡村生活 (Living in the countryside or having an urban life)

The controversial topic with respect to choosing a place to live never fails to attract public attention. There has been a heated debate on whether living in the countryside or having an urban life is more desirable. As far as I am concerned, I tend to be the advocate for urban life.

It is evidently reasonable for me to believe that urban life offers residents great convenience and high efficiency. This is because cities provide seamless transportation, healthcare, and commercial services, significantly improving life efficiency. To illustrate this, my personal experience is a convincing example. When I lived in Shanghai, I could easily access hospitals, supermarkets, and public transit within minutes, allowing me to balance work, study, and leisure effortlessly. As a result, the convenience of city life saves time and energy, enabling a more productive lifestyle.

In contrast, it is commonly believed as a fact that rural areas' natural environments reduce stress and promote long-term well-being, and rural living is more affordable. Nevertheless, this argument does not hold water in the context of modern life. It should be pointed out that cities offer irreplaceable advantages for personal and professional growth, especially for young people. That is to say, while rural life may suit retirement or short-term relaxation, urban centers remain crucial for achieving long-term goals.

In a nutshell, due to the mentioned reasons, we may safely reach the conclusion that urban life is preferable for its convenience, career opportunities, and educational resources. Although countryside living has its merits, the dynamic and efficient nature of cities better supports modern aspirations and daily needs.
